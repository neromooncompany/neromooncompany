# Neromoon

**AI products for business.** Tashkent, Uzbekistan · est. 2026

Neromoon is a technology company that builds AI products for small and medium businesses. Our goal is simple: let one person do what used to take a whole team — starting with the business's presence on the web.

We build for Uzbekistan and the region first — with the languages, payments and channels people actually use — and design every product to work anywhere.
